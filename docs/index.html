vs Prior Guidance

Why Happening
Revenue growth driven by strong European and defence demand, conversion of pipeline, and ramp of vehicle-mountable counter-drone solutions. Margin compression driven by: (1) higher mix of pass-through third-party hardware in contracts (structurally dilutive), (2) AUD/USD or other currency movements, (3) one-off raw material impairment during production facility relocation and ERP implementation. The third-party hardware pass-through is a recurring structural feature of reseller contracts and is likely to recur, not a clean one-off.
Balance Sheet
No balance sheet data provided. CEO references ongoing investment in R&D, sales capability, systems, and 'working capital that growth at this pace requires' — a clear signal of cash consumption. Rapid revenue growth with margin compression and heavy capex/opex investment raises the question of whether existing cash reserves (from prior equity raises) remain sufficient or whether another raise is approaching. No explicit guidance on cash position or funding needs.
Red Flags
  • Gross margin dropped 500bps to 60% in 1H — management targets 65% for full year implying a very sharp 2H recovery; this is mathematically aggressive and not adequately explained
  • Third-party hardware pass-through in contracts is structurally margin-dilutive and likely to persist as European reseller deals grow — not a clean one-off
  • Zero profitability metrics disclosed (no EBITDA, no net profit, no operating cash flow) despite calling this a 'trading update' — significant omission for a company of this scale
  • FY2027+ committed revenue of only $26M is thin for a business guiding $250-270M in FY2026, suggesting very limited revenue visibility beyond the current year
  • Recurring revenue is only 11.3% of 1H revenue — the subscription/software model is still nascent despite being central to the valuation thesis
  • Leadership transition in April referenced but not explained; post-transition 'detailed organisational assessment' hints at prior execution issues
  • Raw material impairment during ERP and facility relocation is an operational execution flag — scale-up is not running cleanly
  • FY2026 revenue guidance of $250-270M requires $124-144M of 2H revenue against $125.8M in 1H — back-half weighting acknowledged but reliant on orders 'not currently tracked'
Bottom Line
Revenue growth is genuinely strong (+74% 1H YoY, $206M committed with 5 months remaining) and the demand environment is real, but the 500bps gross margin miss is the key negative and management's framing as largely transitory is not convincing given structural third-party hardware mix in contracts. The complete absence of profitability and cash flow data in a 'trading update' for a company burning working capital at scale is a material omission investors should push hard on at the 26 August result.
