## What this is

Brambles is not an investment I analysed; it is where I worked, and the shares
came from the staff plan: **buy one, get one free, capped at A$5,000 a year.** I
took that deal every year I was there, with the purchases made at the end of
each month, and I have held the lot for a very long time since.

The headline return looks excellent, and it is — but the honest source of it is
the **100% match** on money I was contributing anyway, plus more than a decade
and a half of compounding. That is the plan and time doing the work, not me
picking a winner.

## A note on the data

The ledger shows two Brambles decisions. That is not what actually happened —
the real history is dozens of small month-end plan purchases across the years I
was employed, accumulated here into two lines. The dates and prices are
therefore representative rather than exact, and the holding was **halved in a
divorce** along the way. None of that changes what the card is for.

## Why it is a Trophy Asset

Like the Broncos, this is held for a reason that is not return. I will not sell
it: I want the boys to inherit it one day and know their dad worked at Brambles.
That is a perfectly good reason to own something and a terrible reason to pretend
it is still a live investment decision — so the card says plainly which one it
is, and the pre-mortem watches for the day sentiment starts masquerading as
analysis.
