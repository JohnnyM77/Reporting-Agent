## What this is

Rolls-Royce is not a company I analysed or chose; it is where I worked, and the
shares came as a bonus. I have kept them, and I intend to keep them.

Like Brambles and like the Broncos, the reason is not return. I want the boys to
inherit these one day and know their dad worked at Rolls-Royce. That is the
whole card. The pre-mortem exists only to keep me honest about the difference
between a memento I am choosing to keep and capital I am simply failing to
manage.
