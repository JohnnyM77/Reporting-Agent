## Why this position exists

RAS Technology — Racing and Sports, ticker RTH — is the data engine behind
global horse racing. Not the bookmaker, not the tote: the company that ingests
racing data from a thousand-plus venues across thirty countries and turns it
into the curated form, odds and analytics that everyone else's betting product
runs on. Flutter, Bet365, Entain, Tabcorp, the Hong Kong Jockey Club — they are
customers.

**I can't stand horse racing.** That is not incidental to the thesis, it is
close to the heart of it. I don't need to enjoy the sport or approve of the
gambling; I need to notice that it is an enormous, century-old, sticky global
industry, and that RAS sells the one thing every participant in it needs. It is
the purest pick-and-shovel play I own: indifferent to which horse wins, paid
whenever anyone bets.

The whole idea came from the Strawman community — mushroompanda and Wini's
deep-dives, then jcmleng's full thesis and risk table posted on 23 October
2025, two days after I bought. I followed good work by people who had done it.

## The record so far

One parcel, bought at **$0.97 in October 2025**, into pre-announced founder
selling that was capping the price. It is currently well underwater, in the
mid-$0.60s — the tech sell-off plus an FY26 result that, on the surface, looks
like a growth company going backwards. The ledger holds the figures.

## What FY26 actually was

Not a broken thesis — a **deliberate investment year**, which is the single most
important thing to hold onto when looking at an ugly print.

Revenue grew 34%. Earnings went backwards, into a small normalised loss, because
the company spent hard on UK expansion, Asia, new products and the LeoVegas
build, and those costs arrived before the revenue they were meant to unlock. The
LeoVegas UK brands only migrated *after* year-end, so the ARR they bring isn't
in these numbers. TAB re-signed for four years. Stake, lost, was more than
replaced.

So FY26 is operating leverage running in reverse on purpose. My own words on the
1H result back in February were the right frame: *"if operating leverage
materialises in FY27 this could look smart… execution over the next 12–18 months
will determine whether this becomes a high-margin platform business — or a
perpetual jam-tomorrow story."* FY26 didn't answer that question. FY27 will.

## The pillar under strain, and why it's the right one

**P3 — high-margin, self-funding growth — is now STRAINED, and nothing else is.**
That is the honest read. The moat (P1) and the industry (P2) are untouched by a
spending year; what a spending year tests is whether the growth still funds
itself. Right now it doesn't: free cash flow is negative, cash is down to about
$4.2m, and there is no disclosed debt facility. One more year like this and the
P3 kill condition — a raise — fires.

That is why the resolution date is the FY27 result and the pre-mortem leads with
the balance sheet. The bet was always that a boring data monopoly would compound
quietly; FY26 turned it, for now, into a race between operating leverage and the
cash balance.
