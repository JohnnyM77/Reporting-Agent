## What this is

VEU is the international leg — thousands of companies across the developed and
emerging world *outside* the US, in one cheap line. It exists so the portfolio
is not purely a bet on Australia's small, concentrated, commodity-and-banks
market.

Like the other two ETFs it has been **fine rather than spectacular**, which is
the point of a diversifier.

## The deliberate gap worth remembering

The "ex-US" is not an accident, and it is the one thing to keep in mind: this
fund leaves out the largest and, over the past decade, best-performing market in
the world. That was a reasonable way to slice global exposure when I already had
US-linked exposure elsewhere — but it does mean VEU on its own is only half a
global allocation, and it means my international return skipped the strongest
market of the era.

## Why Grade C

No research document, none intended — buying a broad index is a decision not to
analyse. Reconstructed from memory and the ledger, which is Grade C by
definition, and entirely appropriate for what this holding is.
